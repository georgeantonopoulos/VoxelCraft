# The Keeper's Path — progression and mythos (proposal)

Status: **draft for approval** (updated 2026-09-26). The journey below marks what exists and what is proposed.

## Where we are (updated 2026-09-26)

Built and playable today:
- **Gather**: sticks (under every wood-bearing tree), stones, flint shards (knapped by striking stone on stone), Lumina flora (glowing in dark places), logs (sawn from felled trees) and planks (split logs).
- **Craft**: stick + bindings bench. Stones/shards on the right slots make a pick, axe, pick-axe, saw, spear, maul, or a Lumina wand (flora on the tip).
- **Build**: carry logs and planks, set them upright as posts or lying as walls (corners notch), planks as floors and pitched roofs.
- **Survive**: a slow weather cycle. Rain puts out fires and torches left in the open; a roof keeps them burning. Campfires from sticks around a stone.
- **Restore**: Root Hollows (broken ancient oak stumps) in mild clearings of every land. A rootling leads you to the nearest; one flora offered beside it wakes it; its tree grows back.
- **Goal**: an 11-step quest line (gather, craft, find a hollow, restore it, fell trees, place torches, see biomes, survive a night, restore 3), then endless "Renewal" tiers. Ranks from essence give a faster stride.
- **Worlds**: five lands (Grove, Sky Archipelago, Frozen Wastes, Lush Jungle, Chaos), saved separately with their own inventory, builds and progress.

What is still missing: after the first hollow the goal never asks more of you, the resources you build up have no purpose in the goal, the building has one reason (rain) but no place to belong, and the story is one line per quest.

## The player's journey (what a player does, in order)

This is the arc the loop below produces. Parts marked **(new)** are proposed; everything else exists.

**1. Waking (first 10 minutes).** You wake in a faded glade with empty hands. The quest tracker asks for fallen sticks, then stones. Striking stone on stone flakes off a shard. At the bench you bind a flake to a stick: your first tool. A rootling watches from the edge of the clearing.

**2. The first hollow (10-25 min).** The Lumina Sense compass stirs. The rootling runs ahead and waits for you; you follow it through the land to a broken, hollow stump in a drained clearing. Lumina flora glows only in dark places (caves, gaps under stone, shaded nooks), so you go looking. You set one flora beside the hollow: it drinks the light, a tree of light grows from it, and you hear the first verse **(new)**.

**3. The first rain (25-45 min).** The sky greys; a slow rain starts and your campfire hisses out. You fell a tree, saw it into logs, and raise posts and a roof. Under the roof, fire survives the rain. This becomes your camp, the **Keeper's Hearth** **(new)**: where you wake, where you keep things, and where verses are read.

**4. The second and third hollows (45 min - 2 h).** Each hollow asks more: three flora carried in an offering bowl, then flora plus a heartstone from the deep obsidian caves **(new)**. The land answers: grass greens and birds return around each woken hollow **(new, local)**, and each gives a gift: stride, night-sight, deep sense or soft step **(new)**.

**5. The Weave (2-5 h).** You set **Wayshrines** **(new)** on hilltops between hollows. When two woken hollows are both within reach of your shrines, a thread of light joins them across the land: you can see the network you are rebuilding.

**6. The Heart (the ending).** With seven hollows joined, the compass points to the **Heart**, a vast dead tree at the oldest grove. You carry the network's light to it; the threads converge, the Heart fills with light, and colour returns to the whole world. The game continues (renewal, building, other lands), but the story has an ending.

## Part I — the loop

**Gather → Craft → Build → Restore → the world answers → you can reach further.**

Each restored hollow must *cost* more than the last and *give back* something that lets you reach the next one. That is the whole design in one sentence.

### 1. Gather (what the land gives)

| Resource | Where | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| Sticks, stones | everywhere | hands |
| Shards | smashing stones on stone | hands |
| **Timber** (new) | felling trees | axe |
| **Resin** (new) | tapping old trees (hold use on a trunk) | shard blade |
| **Fibre** (new) | tall grass | hands |
| Lumina flora | caves, dark hollows | hands |
| **Heartstone** (new, rare) | deep caves, near obsidian | pick |

Timber, resin and fibre give the axe and the knife a real purpose. Heartstone makes caves worth descending into.

### 2. Craft (what you shape)

Keep the physical stick-and-bindings bench. Add a few purposeful recipes:

- **Lantern** (stick + flora + resin): carried light that also widens Lumina Sense.
- **Offering bowl** (stone + resin): holds flora; needed for hollows after the first.
- **Rope** (fibre ×3): used in building.

### 3. Build (where you belong)

One small, readable building system, not free-form blocks:

- **Keeper's Hearth** (stones + timber): your camp. Sets your waking point, holds your things, and is where the Grove's story is told (see the Folklore below). It **grows in tiers** as you restore hollows: a fire ring, then a lean-to, then a lodge.
- **Wayshrines** (stone + timber + one flora): placed anywhere. A small light that marks a path and lets Lumina Sense "see" further from it.
- **Lumina Threads**: once two restored hollows are both within reach of your Wayshrines, a visible thread of light joins them. The threads *are* the network you are rebuilding.

### 4. Restore (the goal, with rising cost)

| Hollow | Offering | What changes when it wakes |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | 1 flora | The hollow's tree grows; the area greens; you learn the first verse |
| 2nd | 3 flora in an offering bowl | Wildlife returns nearby; Lantern recipe revealed |
| 3rd | 5 flora + 1 heartstone | Lumina Sense reaches twice as far; Hearth tier 2 |
| 4th+ | flora + heartstone + a Wayshrine within 40 m | Threads join the network; new verse each time |
| The Heart | a thread network linking 7 hollows | The endgame: the Heart Tree wakes (below) |

**The world answers.** Each hollow restores colour and life around it (grass greens, flora regrow, birds return), so progress is *visible in the land*, not just on the HUD. The vitality grade already does this globally; this makes it local and permanent.

### 5. Improve (you grow too)

Ranks already give Keeper's Stride. Replace generic ranks with **gifts from each restored hollow**, chosen from a small set:

- Stride (walk faster)
- Night-sight (the Keeper's glow reaches further)
- Deep Sense (Lumina Sense also shows heartstone)
- Soft Step (wildlife no longer flees)

### Endgame: the Heart Tree

When the network links seven hollows, the compass points to the **Heart**, an enormous dead tree at the world's oldest Sacred Grove. Bringing the network's light to it plays a short, wordless sequence: threads converge, the dead tree fills with light, and the whole world's colour returns at once. The game continues afterwards (renewal, building, exploring), but the story has an ending.

### What the HUD shows

The quest tracker becomes **the next need**, e.g. "The second hollow asks for 3 flora in an offering bowl (you have 1)". The checklist-style tutorial steps remain for the first ~10 minutes only.

## Part II — the mythos (draft)

Told in short verses, never in cutscenes: one verse per restored hollow, read at the Hearth or heard as a whisper when a hollow wakes. The player pieces the story together.

### The world as the Grove remembers it

> Before the first stone cooled, there was the **Lumina**: a light that did not burn, running beneath the soil like water in a root. Where it gathered, things grew. Where it flowed, things were joined.
>
> The trees drank it and grew old and wise. The oldest of them, the **Heart**, drank the most, and through its roots every forest, marsh and mountain could feel every other. That joining was called the **Weave**.

### The Keepers

> The Weave did not tend itself. Those who listened to it were called **Keepers**. They carried no crowns and built no walls. They walked the long roads between groves, cleared the dead wood, lit the dark places, and sang to the roots when the roots were tired.
>
> Their only rule: *take what is fallen, give back what is living.*

### The Fading

> In a hard age the Keepers grew few, and the people who came after them wanted the light for themselves. They cut into the roots to draw it up, and lit their halls with it, and the halls were very bright.
>
> The Weave bled out through those cuts. One by one the groves went quiet. The **Root Hollows** you find now are the old wells where the Lumina used to rise, hollowed stumps dreaming of what they were. The halls are gone; only their obsidian floors remain, deep underground where the light was burned.

(This is why deep caves hold obsidian and heartstone: the scars of the old burning.)

### The Rootlings

> Not everything forgot. The **Rootlings** are the Weave's last memory of the Keepers: small wood-spirits who wait by the paths, and lead anyone who listens to the nearest sleeping hollow. They do not speak. They remember the way.

### You

> You wake in the last flicker of the last grove, with nothing but your hands. No one tells you who you were. The Rootling waits. The compass stirs.
>
> The Grove does not need a hero. It needs someone to take what is fallen and give back what is living, long enough for the Weave to remember itself.

### The ending

> When seven hollows sing to each other again, the Heart hears them. It has been dead for an age, and it is not dead.

### Where the story lives in the game

- **Verses**: one per restored hollow (a quiet line of text plus the discovery motif). They collect in a **Book of the Grove**, read at the Hearth.
- **Places**: obsidian hall floors in deep caves (the old burning), Wayshrine ruins on hilltops (old Keeper roads), and the Heart at the oldest grove.
- **Names used everywhere**: Lumina, the Weave, Keepers, Root Hollows, Rootlings, the Heart, the Fading. The HUD and quests switch to these words (e.g. "Lumina Sense" stays; "vitality" becomes "the Weave").

## Build order (if approved)

1. Offering costs per hollow, "next need" tracker, and the Book of the Grove with the first four verses. (Small; changes the feel of the goal immediately.)
2. Timber, resin and fibre; Lantern and Offering Bowl recipes.
3. Keeper's Hearth (placement, waking point, tiers).
4. Local restoration (greening and regrowth around each hollow) and hollow gifts.
5. Wayshrines and Lumina Threads.
6. Heartstone and deep caves; the Heart Tree endgame.

Each step ships on its own and is playable.
